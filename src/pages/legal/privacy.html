Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026.

This policy explains what Otoro Club LLC (“Otoro Club,” “we”) collects when you contact us, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We are a private event catering service in La Jolla, California.

What we collect

When you submit an inquiry, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, phone number (if given), the venue, the event date, the estimated guest count, any dietary notes, how you found us, and anything you write in the message field. If you email us directly, we receive whatever you include.

Why we collect it

We use this information to respond to your inquiry, plan and quote an engagement, coordinate the event, and keep records of our bookings. We do not sell your information.

How it is handled

Inquiries are delivered to us by email through a form-processing service and stored in our email and business records. We use hosting and analytics providers to run this site.

  • Form delivery: FormSubmit (delivers the inquiry to our inbox).
  • Hosting: Vercel.
  • Analytics: Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights, loaded only after you accept cookies.
